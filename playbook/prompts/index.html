Role

Task

Explain [Topic] so that I understand it the first time.

Context

I am in [Class or year]. What I already know: [What you already know]. The part that confuses me: [The confusing part].

Format

Reply in English. Use under 150 words and one everyday example from Indian life. End with 3 short questions to check if I understood.
